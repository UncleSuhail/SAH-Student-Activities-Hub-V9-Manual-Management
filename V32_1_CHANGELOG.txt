SAH V32.1 — Annual Budget Live Deduction
================================================
Implemented:
- The Dean-entered annual budget is the authoritative ceiling regardless of its amount.
- Spent Budget is calculated from approved activities across all platform departments.
- Remaining Annual Budget = Dean Annual Budget - Approved/Spent Budget.
- Every newly approved activity immediately reduces the remaining annual budget.
- Rejected and pending requests do not reduce the annual balance.
- The calculation applies to existing localStorage activities as well as new approvals.
- The annual-budget circle updates immediately after approval or budget editing.
- JavaScript syntax validation passed.
